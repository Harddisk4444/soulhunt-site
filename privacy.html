This website

This site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics. The embedded video is served by YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); YouTube's own policy applies once you play it. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

The game

Soul Hunt runs on Roblox. Your account, purchases and chat are handled by Roblox under Roblox's privacy policy. The game itself stores only your in-game progress (level, Rings, items, settings) against your Roblox user id, and records gameplay events through Roblox's own analytics to balance the game. Nothing is sold or shared outside Roblox.

Contact

Bugs, questions, takedown requests: the Discord server (#help or #bug-reports), or the GGSK page on Facebook.

Developer: GGSK (Money Talks Studio on Roblox). Soul Hunt is an original work; names, characters and art are the developer's own.
